🔄 Return & Exchange Policy

Return Window

We offer a 7-day return/exchange window from the date of delivery. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help.

✅ Eligible for Return/Exchange

  • Wrong product delivered (different from what you ordered)
  • Defective or damaged product upon arrival
  • Size doesn't match (exchange only)
  • Product significantly different from website description

❌ Not Eligible for Return

  • Products used, worn, washed, or altered
  • Products without original tags and packaging
  • Innerwear/underwear (due to hygiene reasons)
  • Products returned after 7 days of delivery
  • Products purchased during Stock Clearance sale

How to Request a Return

  • Step 1: Contact us via WhatsApp or Phone within 7 days of delivery
  • Step 2: Share your Order ID and photos of the product
  • Step 3: We'll review and approve your request within 24 hours
  • Step 4: Ship the product back to us (customer bears return shipping cost)
  • Step 5: Once received, we'll process your exchange or refund within 3-5 business days

Return Shipping

Return shipping charges are to be borne by the customer, unless the return is due to our mistake (wrong item shipped or defective product).

Refund Policy

  • Refunds will be processed via bKash or Nagad within 3-5 business days after we receive the returned product
  • For COD orders, refund will be sent to your preferred mobile banking number
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable
